Assalam o Alaikum! Welcome to AFS Virtual Tutor Hub – Pakistan's most trusted free online tutoring platform. We are delighted to have you and your child as part of our learning community. This document outlines the terms and guidelines governing student and parent participation.

1. Welcome to AFS Virtual Tutor Hub

We are delighted to have you and your child as part of our learning community. We are committed to fostering academic excellence with integrity.

Our Promise to You:

  • We provide verified, manually reviewed expert tutors across all educational curricula.
  • Your child's safety, security, and academic satisfaction are our highest priority.
  • We are committed to creating a positive, secure, and effective learning environment for every student.

2. Our Commitment to You

AFS Virtual Tutor Hub is built on the foundation of trust, honesty, and quality education. We are dedicated to:

  • Quality Assurance: Every tutor on our platform is personally verified by AFS Management through a rigorous screening process.
  • Transparency: Clear communication regarding tuition fees, schedule policies, and expectations.
  • Student Safety: Strict operational protocols to maintain a safe online learning space.
  • Educational Excellence: Focus on concept-based learning, critical thinking, and practical application.
  • Parent Partnership: Keeping parents informed and actively engaged in their child's development.

3. Free 2-Day Demo Trial

Every student is entitled to a free 2-day demo trial with their selected tutor — completely free with no advance commitment required.

How It Works:

  • Select a qualified tutor from our verified faculty listing.
  • Attend 2 live demo sessions (one session per day) to evaluate the tutor's methodology.
  • Decide if the teacher's style aligns with your child's learning needs.

After the Demo Period:

Upon completing the demo trial and choosing to proceed, parents and students agree to follow AFS terms regarding fee payments, punctuality, ethics, and session schedules.

4. Your Rights – Replacement & Refund Policy

We prioritize student satisfaction. If you encounter any issues with your tutor, follow this resolution workflow:

Step Action Required
Step 1 Inform AFS Management or the Director immediately via WhatsApp if you have concerns regarding teaching quality or comprehension.
Step 2 AFS Management will evaluate the class. If improvement is not observed, we will replace the instructor with another qualified tutor.
Step 3 If you remain unsatisfied within 7 days of receiving the replacement tutor, AFS will issue a 70% refund of the paid amount.

Important Refund Notes:

  • No refunds are issued after 7 days from tutor assignment as tutors are compensated in advance.
  • Refund requests must be formally submitted in writing to AFS Management.
  • Approved refunds are processed within 7–10 business days.

5. Your Responsibilities as a Student/Parent

5.1 Active Participation & Conduct

  • Attend all scheduled classes regularly and on time.
  • Participate actively in lessons and complete assigned homework or quizzes.
  • Treat tutors and AFS staff with respect, good manners, and professional courtesy.
  • Ensure students maintain appropriate digital decorum during live sessions.

5.2 Technical Requirements

  • Maintain a stable internet connection for seamless video and audio streaming.
  • Ensure access to a computer, laptop, or tablet equipped with a working camera and microphone.
  • Provide a quiet, distraction-free environment for learning.

5.3 Session Cancellations

Provide at least 24 hours' advance notice for any class cancellation. Cancellations without notice may result in the session being counted as completed.

6. Confidentiality & Privacy

  • Your tutor's personal contact details are private and provided solely for educational coordination.
  • You must not share tutor contact details externally or use them for non-educational purposes.
  • Keep all communications focused strictly on academic subjects and scheduling.
  • Student data is protected under our strict Privacy Policy and will not be shared with unauthorized third parties.

7. Student Support & Communication

7.1 Direct Messaging & Resources

Students can message tutors via the website or designated group chats for academic queries, notes, and study resources. Tutors are expected to respond within 24 hours.

7.2 Regular Updates & MCQs

Students receive access to free practice MCQs, test series, scholarship announcements, and educational resources directly through the AFS platform.

8. Fee Payment & Collection

  • Free Registration: Account creation on AFS Virtual Tutor Hub is 100% free with zero hidden platform charges.
  • Direct Tuition Fee: Fees are agreed upon between the parent/student and the tutor. AFS charges no extra commission to parents.
  • Payment Schedule: Fees are collected monthly in advance via Easypaisa, JazzCash, or direct Bank Transfer as guided by AFS Management.
  • Late Payment: Payments delayed by more than 7 days from the due date may lead to session suspension.

9. Session Cancellations & Rescheduling

Maintain a regular timetable to ensure continuous academic progress. Rescheduling requests require a 24-hour advance notice and are subject to teacher availability.

10. Rules & Legal Compliance

We strictly enforce platform integrity and student-teacher safety.

Disrespectful behavior, sharing private tutor information, non-payment, or unethical conduct will lead to account suspension. Where necessary, legal proceedings may be initiated under the governing laws of Pakistan.

11. Safety & Well-being

All tutors undergo strict background checks. Parents are encouraged to observe online classes periodically to monitor safety and engagement. Any safety concern should be reported immediately to AFS Management.

12. General Provisions

  • No Guarantee of Results: AFS does not guarantee specific exam marks or grades, as student outcomes depend on individual student effort and practice.
  • Governing Law: These terms are governed and interpreted in accordance with the laws of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan.

13. Educational Philosophy & Quality Commitment

AFS is not merely a tutoring website; it is a quality-driven educational ecosystem. We focus on concept mastery, critical thinking, practical learning, and character building.

Overall Principle: Building a trustworthy, professional, and student-centered system focused on measurable student growth and parental trust.

14. Agreement & Acknowledgement

By registering or using our platform, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agreed to all student and parent terms and conditions stated above.

"At AFS, trust is our foundation. By working together with honesty and respect - students, tutors, staff, and parents – we can empower students to reach their full potential. We believe in you, and together, we can create a thriving online learning environment where everyone succeeds."
