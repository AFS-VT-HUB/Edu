Section 1: Student & Course Information
01Is AFS Virtual Tutor Hub free for students?

Yes! Registration, browsing teachers, and requesting demo classes are completely free for students. You only pay the tutor's agreed fee after the free demo and when you decide to start regular classes.

AFS Philosophy: We believe that every student deserves access to quality education regardless of financial situation. This is why our platform remains free to explore.
02How do I sign up as a student?

Go to the "Student Registration" page, fill in your personal and academic information, read and accept the Terms & Conditions, and click Register. Fields marked with * are mandatory. You will receive a confirmation email after successful registration.

03How can I search for a tutor?

Go to the "Find Teachers" page and use our filters to search by subject, curriculum, experience, and rating. You can browse teacher profiles and contact AFS management regarding the tutor(s) via WhatsApp.

04How can I hire a tutor?

After finding a suitable tutor, click the "Request Free Demo" button. You will get a free 2-day demo class. If satisfied, you can continue with regular classes by arranging payment and schedule directly with the tutor.

05What is the free demo class?

Every student is entitled to a free 2-day demo trial with their chosen tutor. During these 2 days, you can assess the tutor's teaching style and decide if they are the right fit. No commitment or payment is required.

06What types of courses and curricula do you cover?

We cover all major curricula, including:

  • Pakistani Boards: Federal Board (FBISE), Punjab, Sindh, KPK, Balochistan, AKU-EB
  • Cambridge: O Levels, A Levels, IGCSE, GCSE
  • International: IB, Edexcel, Indian Curriculum (CBSE/ICSE), Gulf Curriculum
  • Competitive Exams: CSS, PMS, ISSB, MDCAT, ECAT, SAT, GRE, IELTS, Cadet College
  • Other: Madrassa/Islamic Studies, Specialized Exams
07What subjects do you offer?

We cover all major subjects, including Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, Urdu, Islamiat, Pakistan Studies, Computer Science, Accounting, Economics, Business Studies, Statistics, Sociology, Psychology, Law, Arabic, Biomaths, Further Maths, and more.

08Do you offer physical home tuitions or only online classes?

We offer both!

  • Online Tutoring: Available worldwide
  • In-Person Home/On-Campus Tutoring: Available exclusively in Peshawar only
09Can my child take classes from outside Pakistan?

Yes! Our online tutoring services are available worldwide. Students in the UK, USA, UAE, KSA, Canada, Europe, and other countries can easily connect with our verified tutors who understand Pakistani and international curricula.

10Is online tutoring safe for my child?

Yes! All our tutors are verified by AFS Management. We conduct background checks and manual profile reviews. Parents can also observe classes, and we have clear policies for professional conduct and student safety.

11How are tutors verified and qualified?

Every tutor is manually reviewed and verified by AFS Management. We check:

  • Academic qualifications and degrees
  • Teaching experience
  • Identity documents (CNIC)
  • Subject expertise
  • Student reviews and ratings
AFS Quality Standard: We believe in measurable student development, not just conducting classes. Our tutors are trained to be professional digital educators.
12How and where do online classes take place?

Classes take place via Zoom or Google Meet. Tutors use digital whiteboards and screen sharing for an interactive learning experience. You will receive a class link before each session.

13What are the service charges for students/parents?

There are no registration or matching fees for students and parents. You only pay the tutor's agreed fee. AFS charges a commission from the tutor, not from you.

14How do I ask a question or get support?

You can:

  • Message your tutor directly at any time after class
  • Contact AFS Management via WhatsApp
  • Email us: afsvirtualtutorhub369@gmail.com
  • Use the contact form on our website

Response Time: We respond within 24 hours.

15Are there crash courses or special exam prep sessions available?

Yes! We offer specialized exam preparation for MDCAT, ECAT, SAT, GRE, IELTS, CSS, PMS, ISSB, Cadet College, and university admission tests. You can request a tutor specifically for exam preparation.

16Does AFS Virtual Tutor Hub provide scholarships for deserving students?

We occasionally offer scholarships and discounts for deserving students. Follow our website and social media for updates on scholarship opportunities and announcements.

17What if I'm not satisfied with my tutor?

If you are not satisfied, follow this process:

  • 1. Inform AFS Management immediately via WhatsApp
  • 2. We will observe the situation
  • 3. If the tutor does not improve, we will replace them with another qualified instructor
  • 4. If still not satisfied within 7 days of receiving the new tutor, AFS will refund 70% of your paid amount
  • 5. No refund after 7 days, as tutors are paid in advance
18Can I change my tutor if I'm not satisfied?

Yes, you can request a tutor change through AFS Management. We will match you with a new tutor based on your requirements.

19What if I need to reschedule a session?

You can discuss rescheduling directly with your tutor. Please provide reasonable notice (at least 24 hours) for any cancellation or rescheduling.

20How do the fees work?

Fees are agreed upon between you and the tutor. Payment is typically collected monthly in advance. The fee depends on the tutor's qualifications, experience, and the student's grade and subject requirements.

21What is the AFS educational philosophy?

AFS is not intended to be simply a tutoring website. Our long-term purpose is to build a professional, trustworthy, quality-focused educational platform. Our approach emphasizes:

  • Concept-based learning rather than memorization
  • Student-centered learning where the child is the focus
  • Critical thinking and problem-solving as core skills
  • Exam preparation with effective strategies
  • Practical learning with real-life applications
  • Digital learning and integration of technology
  • Communication skills development
  • Character development, ethics, manners and honesty
  • Regular feedback and continuous improvement
22How does AFS track student progress?

AFS focuses on measurable student development rather than simply conducting classes. We track:

  • Quiz performance
  • Assignment completion
  • Topics/syllabus covered
  • Student progress over time
  • Student satisfaction
  • Tutor performance

We implement standardized class structures and weekly reporting to ensure consistency and quality.

23What makes AFS different from other tutoring platforms?

AFS stands out because:

  • 100% free for students to browse and request demos
  • Manually verified teachers by AFS Management
  • Free MCQ tests and practice materials
  • 2-day free demo before any commitment
  • Global online and Peshawar home tutoring options
  • All curricula covered under one platform
  • Educational philosophy focused on concept-based, student-centered learning
  • Character and value building alongside academics
  • Regular progress reports for parents
  • Professional development for tutors
Section 2: Teacher Registration
01Are the teachers on AFS Virtual Tutor Hub verified?

Yes! AFS Management manually verifies all teachers. We check academic qualifications, experience, identity documents, and conduct background checks before approving any tutor profile.

02What is the minimum qualification required to register as a teacher?

You must have at least a Bachelor's degree in your subject area. Additional qualifications (Master's, MPhil, PhD) and teaching experience are highly valued.

03How does the teacher registration pipeline work?

The registration process is:

  • 1. Fill Application Form: Complete all sections with accurate information
  • 2. Upload Documents: Submit your CV, educational certificates, and CNIC
  • 3. Verification: AFS Management reviews your application and documents
  • 4. Approval: If approved, you will receive a confirmation and be added to our teacher directory
  • 5. Start Teaching: Students can now find and contact you for tutoring
04How to sign up as a tutor?

Go to the "Teacher Registration" page, fill in all required information (marked with *), upload your CV and documents, read and accept the Terms & Conditions, and submit your application. You will receive a response within 24-48 hours.

05What is the complete tutor registration process?

The complete process includes:

  • 1. Personal Information: Full name, father's name, date of birth, contact details
  • 2. Qualification: Educational background with marks/GPA
  • 3. Subject Expertise: Subjects you are qualified to teach
  • 4. Experience: Years of teaching experience
  • 5. Curriculum Experience: Curricula you have taught
  • 6. CV Upload: Submit your updated CV (PDF, <10MB)
  • 7. Technology Setup: Internet connection, workspace, devices
  • 8. Internet Details: Connection type, speed, backup plan
  • 9. Teaching Approach: Why you are a perfect fit for AFS
  • 10. Terms & Conditions: Accept the agreement
06How to upload my documents and CNIC?

During the registration process, you will find a file upload section. Upload your:

  • CV/Resume (PDF format, less than 10MB)
  • Educational certificates (optional)
  • CNIC (required for verification)
07What if I do not provide educational documents?

Your application will be delayed or may be cancelled. Documents are mandatory for verification. Please ensure all required documents are uploaded.

08When will I appear in the search results?

After AFS Management approves your application, your profile will appear in the search results. Approval usually takes 24-48 hours.

09I have completed my tutor registration. What should I do next?

After registration:

  • 1. Wait for your application to be reviewed and approved (24-48 hours)
  • 2. Once approved, you will receive a confirmation
  • 3. Complete your profile with a clear photo and detailed description
  • 4. Be active and responsive to student inquiries
  • 5. Share daily class links in the AFS management group
10What are the service charges for tutors?

Your exact commission/percentage will be communicated to you privately on WhatsApp after approval.

11When will I receive my payment?

You will receive payment after successful completion of 3 weeks of tuition. If you withdraw before completing 3 weeks, no fee will be paid to you.

12Can I teach online?

Yes! All tutoring on AFS is primarily online via Zoom or Google Meet. We also offer in-person home tutoring exclusively in Peshawar.

13What tools do I use for online tutoring?

You can use:

  • Zoom or Google Meet for video classes
  • Digital whiteboards for interactive teaching
  • Screen sharing for presentations and resources
  • WhatsApp for communication with students
14What is the difference between Local Tuitions and International Tuitions?
  • Local/Regular Tuitions: Standard tuitions for Pakistani students, subject to standard commission terms
  • International/Premium Tuitions: High-value tuitions for international students (UK, USA, Canada, UAE, etc.), often with specific requirements and a tiered commission structure
